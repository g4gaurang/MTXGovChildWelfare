The product

A system of work for the people responsible for progress.

Child welfare work crosses teams, organizations, policy rules, and time. The product gives practitioners, supervisors, providers, legal partners, and program leaders role-appropriate experiences on a shared data and workflow foundation.

01
◎

For practitioners

Guided work, fewer duplicate entries, mobile access, and a single view of plans, services, visits, and decisions.

02
◇

For supervisors

Workload, deadlines, approvals, risk signals, and case progress surfaced where coaching and review happen.

03
⌂

For providers & families

Purpose-built portals for referrals, availability, documents, status, communication, and progress updates.

04
▦

For program leaders

Operational and outcome measures that reveal service gaps, placement pressure, and improvement opportunities.

Product capabilities

Start with the capability that matters now. Expand on the same foundation.

Each module can be configured around agency practice and connected through defined interfaces and shared data.

Identify and understand

Bring reports, screening, assessment, and investigation into a guided flow.

Capture people and allegation information once, route work by policy, and organize safety and risk information for authorized review.

  • Intake & hotlineGuided reporting, screening and referral decisions
  • Assessment & investigationTasks, evidence, contacts and findings
  • Risk managementStructured assessment with professional oversight

Plan and coordinate

Keep the case plan connected to the work happening around it.

Coordinate goals, services, contacts, approvals, visits, documents, and progress through configurable workflows.

  • Case managementPlans, tasks, contacts, notes and reviews
  • Service deliveryService authorization, progress and completion
  • Mobile practiceField-ready access for timely documentation

Stabilize and advance

Support placement decisions and keep permanency work visible.

Connect child needs, placement options, family connections, court milestones, plans, and long-term outcomes.

  • PlacementMatching, availability, history and stability
  • PermanencyReunification, adoption, guardianship and youth transitions
  • Family experienceAppropriate access, communication and participation

Activate the network

See provider capacity and move referrals with less friction.

Manage licensing, credentials, services, availability, referrals, acceptance, delivery, and performance in one connected model.

  • Provider managementProfile, licensing, capacity and quality
  • Referral managementMatching, routing, acceptance and status
  • External portalSecure collaboration and document exchange

Connect the ecosystem

Make finance, courts, eligibility, and partner data part of the product.

Use APIs and governed exchange patterns to reduce isolated data and support a more complete operational view.

  • Eligibility & financeIV-E support, budgets, invoices and payments
  • Courts & legalHearing milestones, documents and bi-directional exchange
  • Data exchangeStandards-aligned APIs, batch files and partner integration

Product architecture

Modernize in modules without creating another island.

Bounded product modules sit on reusable platform services, governed data, and documented interfaces. Agencies can sequence adoption around program priorities and preserve a path for future change.

AI-assisted, not AI-decided

Decision support can help organize information and surface patterns. Authorized staff retain judgment, approval, and accountability.
